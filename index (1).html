Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about your Digital Twin.

How does the AI Twin learn about me?
▼
Your AI Twin learns through multiple channels: your resume, LinkedIn profile, previous writings, voice samples, and direct conversations with you. It uses advanced machine learning to understand your communication style, values, and expertise.
Is my personal data secure?
▼
Yes, we use enterprise-grade encryption and comply with GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulations. Your data is stored securely and never shared with third parties without your explicit consent.
Can I customize how my AI Twin responds?
▼
Absolutely! You can customize tone, response style, specific knowledge domains, and conversation guidelines. Your twin adapts to how you want to be represented.
What happens if I want to delete my AI Twin?
▼
You can delete your AI Twin anytime. All associated data will be permanently removed from our servers within 30 days, in accordance with our data retention policy.
Can I integrate it with my existing website?
▼
Yes! We provide easy-to-implement embed codes for WordPress, Webflow, and custom websites. Our API also allows deep integration for advanced use cases.
